## 2. Audience & likely jobs-to-be-done

These are product hypotheses for website planning, not independently measured customer research.

- **Local groups and friends:** quickly check menu, prices, opening hours, and directions before visiting.
- **Visitors to Sreemangal:** understand where the shop is in relation to a familiar landmark and get navigation with minimal friction.
- **Dessert/snack buyers:** browse appetizing photos, identify signature options, and call to confirm an item.
- **Social-media visitors:** move from Facebook/Instagram to a trustworthy menu/location page.

## 3. Website opportunity

Public directory listings provide some basic information, but a dedicated official website could bring that information together in a clearer, more ownable experience:

1. A menu that the business controls and can update.
2. Clear prices and product photography.
3. Accurate map directions and click-to-call on mobile.
4. Authentic social proof and brand photography.
5. A simple shareable URL for use in social profiles, posts, ads, and printed QR codes.

## 4. Brand / visual clues

A publicly listed shop/menu photograph shows colorful, playful in-store branding with saturated pink/magenta, orange, green, cyan/blue, black, and cream. Use this only as a broad cue for an energetic, youthful direction. Build an original website system; do not reproduce the photographed wall art, mascot, menu design, or any third-party brand identity without permission.

## 5. Open questions for the owner

Get written approval for every item below before production launch:

- Official brand spelling: `Waffled`, `Waffle’d`, or another exact form.
- Exact address, Google Maps pin, and best landmark directions.
- Phone number and whether it supports WhatsApp orders.
- Exact opening hours for each day, including holidays.
- Complete current menu with item names, descriptions, variants, and prices.
- Whether dine-in, takeaway, delivery, pre-order, or group orders are currently available.
- Current official Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and any delivery-platform links.
- Logo files, fonts, brand colors, mascot/illustrations, and usage permissions.
- Which photos can be used on the website and whether the owner approves them.
- Whether customer reviews may be embedded or quoted, and which review source should be linked.
- Preferred language: English, Bangla, or a bilingual experience.
- Domain preference and who will control domain/hosting accounts.

## 7. Guardrails

- Do not state opening hours, pricing, delivery, “best in town”, ingredients, dietary claims, certifications, or customer-rating claims as fact unless the owner confirms them and evidence supports them.
- Do not create fake testimonials or use stock waffle imagery in a way that implies it is food sold by this specific shop.
- If temporary stock imagery is used for a design mockup, mark it as a placeholder in the project and replace it before launch.
