# 05 — Suggested Website Copy (English)

> Draft copy only. Replace every bracketed field and get owner approval before publishing. No unverified claims are intended to be factual.

## Brand voice

Playful, warm, direct, appetizing. Use short sentences and specific food descriptions. Avoid claims like “the best in Sreemangal”, “freshest”, “100% homemade”, or “premium ingredients” unless substantiated and approved.

## SEO / browser title

**Waffled Sreemangal | Waffles, Desserts & Snacks in Sreemangal**

## Meta description

**Explore the menu at Waffled Sreemangal. Check [confirmed menu highlights], opening hours, and directions to our shop in Sreemangal, Bangladesh.**

## Header

Navigation: `Menu` · `Our Story` · `Gallery` · `Visit Us`

CTA: `View Menu`

## Hero

Eyebrow: `WAFFLE TIME, SREEMANGAL`

Headline option A: **Make room for something sweet.**

Headline option B: **Your next waffle moment starts here.**

Supporting copy: **Waffles, snacks, and little moments worth sharing. Take a look at the menu and plan your next stop at Waffled Sreemangal.**

Primary CTA: `Explore the Menu`

Secondary CTA: `Get Directions`

Optional location line (publish only after confirmation): `Find us near [approved landmark], Sreemangal.`

Item layout:

`[ITEM NAME]`  
`[Brief description with verified ingredients / toppings]`  
`৳[PRICE]`

Availability note (optional): `Menu items and availability may change. Call us to confirm today's options.`

## About section

Heading: **A little joy, served your way.**

Copy: **Waffled Sreemangal is a local spot for waffle cravings and easygoing snack breaks. Stop by, explore the menu, and make a little room for something good.**

This positioning is intentionally modest. Edit it to match the owner's true story, services, and desired personality.

## Gallery section

Heading: **A closer look**

Supporting text: **A few snapshots from the Waffled experience.**

Only use authentic, approved photos of the food, shop, team, or customers with consent.

## Social proof section

Heading: **Don't just take our word for it**

Copy: **See the latest customer feedback on Google.**

CTA: `Read Google Reviews`

Use the official Google review link. Do not manually invent star ratings, review counts, or testimonials. If the link or rating cannot be verified, omit rating language.

## Visit section

Copy: **Planning a visit? Check the map for the latest route, or call ahead with a quick question.**

Fields:
- Address: `[owner-confirmed full address]`

- Hours: `[owner-confirmed hours]`

Buttons:
