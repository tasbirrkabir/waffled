# 09 — Asset & Content Collection Checklist

## 1. Required owner assets

### Brand
- [ ] Original logo in SVG or high-resolution transparent PNG.
- [ ] Wordmark spelling and capitalization approved.
- [ ] Any brand font files and license/usage terms.
- [ ] Approved palette or existing brand guide, if available.
- [ ] Permission to use mascot, illustrations, logos, and artwork.

### Menu
- [ ] Latest full menu, preferably editable source or high-resolution image/PDF.
- [ ] Plain-text menu list with exact prices.
- [ ] Variants, sizes, add-ons, combos, and availability notes.
- [ ] Ingredients/descriptions the owner approves for public use.
- [ ] Any allergen/dietary information only where accurate and supportable.

### Photography
- [ ] 3–5 hero-worthy waffle/product images.
- [ ] One clear photo per priority menu item or a consistent set of category images.
- [ ] 2–4 real interior/exterior photographs.
- [ ] Street-front photo showing how a first-time visitor can recognize the shop.
- [ ] Optional team photo with consent.
- [ ] Written permission for every customer photo or identifiable person included.

### Business information
- [ ] Exact address and Google Maps pin.
- [ ] Verified contact phone.
- [ ] Whether phone supports WhatsApp.
- [ ] Confirmed opening hours and holiday-hours process.
- [ ] Official social links.
- [ ] Confirmed dine-in/takeaway/delivery details.
- [ ] Owner-approved short brand story.
- [ ] Google review link and approval for any review-related display.

## 2. Photo brief for the owner

Ask for original photos rather than screenshots from social media. Shoot in daylight or soft continuous light, clean the plate/rim, keep the actual serving size, and take:

1. One hero image of the most recognizable waffle, with space around the subject.
2. One close-up that makes the waffle texture/toppings clear.
3. One top-down menu/product image.
4. One photo with a hand/serve interaction if the owner wants a lifestyle angle.
5. One shop exterior image in daylight.
6. One shop interior image that represents the real guest experience.
7. A photo of any non-waffle menu category actually sold.

Do not alter the product to make it look larger or more richly topped than the real serving.

## 3. File naming and delivery

Use simple filenames, lowercase, hyphen-separated, no spaces:

```text
logo-primary.svg
logo-white.svg
hero-signature-waffle.webp
menu-[item-slug].webp
storefront-daylight.webp
interior-wide.webp
```

For each photo, retain the original. Export responsive variants for the website after the owner approves which photo will be used.

## 4. Rights and provenance

- Prefer original business photos and graphics with written permission.
- Do not remove watermarks or use copyrighted competitor art.
- Do not assume third-party directory/menu photos are cleared for website reuse.
- Keep a note of asset source and permissions in the project repository.
- Use stock photos only in prototypes and label them clearly as temporary placeholders.

## 5. Optional social preview assets

- [ ] Open Graph image at 1200×630, using approved logo and real product photo.
- [ ] Square profile/brand icon if needed.
- [ ] QR code pointing to the final HTTPS domain for menus, table cards, or packaging.
